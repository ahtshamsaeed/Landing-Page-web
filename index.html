What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing is the process of promoting products and services through online channels such as search engines, social media, websites and email.

Can I learn digital marketing without experience?

Yes. Beginners can learn digital marketing step by step, starting with SEO, social media marketing, content creation and online advertising.

What career opportunities are available in Pakistan?

You can explore roles such as SEO specialist, social media manager, digital marketing executive, PPC specialist and content marketer. Opportunities depend on your skills and experience.

Do I need a degree to join the course?

No degree is required. Students and working professionals from all educational backgrounds can join.

Are classes available online?

Yes. Live sessions are combined with recorded lectures and practical assignments for flexible learning.

Will I get job assistance after completion?

You receive portfolio guidance, interview preparation and placement support through our recruitment partner network.
